Questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

How does the free trial work?
You get 30 days on the plan you choose, with no card required. Add a payment method before the trial ends to keep going, and switch plans anytime along the way.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from Settings → Plan & billing, and the change applies right away.
What happens if I hit a plan limit?
Checkout is never blocked, so customers can always pay you. You'll get a heads-up at 80% of any limit, so you can upgrade before it matters.
Why aren't email and SMS part of the plans?
Sending volume varies a lot from business to business. Keeping messaging separate keeps plan prices low for everyone. Receipts, payment links and order updates by email are included on every plan.
What if I send more than my pack?
Your messages keep going and the extra is billed at or . If you regularly go well over, a bigger pack usually works out cheaper. The estimator above always picks the cheapest option.
Can I send email from my own domain?
On Business, yes: connect your domain and send as notifications@yourbusiness.com. Starter and Growth send from notify@centrx.co with your business name as the sender. See how to connect your domain.
Is annual billing available?
Yes. Pay yearly and get two months free: a year.
Do I get my own number for texting?
Yes. US carriers require every business to text from its own registered number, so every SMS pack includes a dedicated toll-free number, and we handle the carrier registration for you. You can upgrade to a local number in your area code, or text from the business number your customers already know. See the options under Email and SMS add-ons, or read how to set up your texting number.
Does Centrx include an AI assistant?
Yes. The Centrx Assistant is built into the panel. Ask it how to do something, get answers from your own sales and customer data, or let it reply to customer texts about hours, orders and pickups. It works within each employee's permissions, and texts it sends count toward your SMS pack.
How is my data kept safe?
Every plan has two-factor sign-in, session control, role-based permissions and an audit log. Stripe and PayPal handle card payments, so card numbers never touch Centrx. Read the privacy policy for the details.
How does the Custom plan work?
Apply from Settings → Plan & billing in your Centrx account. Tell us what you need more of and confirm your business details, such as your EIN and state filing number. Our team verifies the business, usually within two business days, and then sets up limits and pricing that fit. Read the full guide.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans have no contract. Cancel from Settings → Plan & billing whenever you like.
