Questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

Is there a free plan?
Yes. Free costs $0 a month for one person at one location, with up to 50 customers, 25 inventory items, 1 GB of storage and unlimited orders and payment links. On Free, Centrx keeps 5% of each card payment, on top of the payment provider's own fee. Paid plans have no Centrx fee, so once you take more than $780 a month by card, Hobby costs less. Cenbot, automations and add-ons need a paid plan.
Is there a free trial?
No. The Free plan does that job: use Centrx at $0 for as long as you like, then upgrade when you need more people, locations, Cenbot or automations. A paid plan is charged the day you choose it. Cancel anytime and you move back to Free when the period you paid for ends.
What do I get for joining the waitlist?
Centrx opens on November 18. Join the waitlist before then and your first two months on any paid plan are free. Sign up with the email you joined with and we apply it to your account. Join the waitlist.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime from Settings → Plan & billing, and the change applies right away.
What happens if I hit a plan limit?
Checkout is never blocked, so customers can always pay you. You'll get a heads-up at 80% of any limit, so you can upgrade before it matters. If your cloud storage fills up, you can still take orders and payments, you just can't add new photos, videos or files until you free up space or add more.
What counts toward cloud storage?
Files you keep in Centrx: inventory photos, proof photos and videos, customer ID scans, signed agreements, Online Store images, email attachments, voicemail and call recordings. Customers, orders, payments and messages don't count, and neither do receipts, which are made whenever they're needed. Every plan includes an allowance, and on paid plans you can add 100 GB for $5/month whenever you need more.
Why aren't email and SMS part of the plans?
Sending volume varies a lot from business to business. Keeping messaging separate keeps plan prices low for everyone. Receipts, payment links and order updates by email are included on every plan.
What if I send more than my pack?
Your messages keep going and the extra is billed at or . If you regularly go well over, a bigger pack usually works out cheaper. The estimator above always picks the cheapest option.
Can I send email from my own domain?
Yes, with Business Email: $9/month for each domain you connect, on any paid plan. You send as notifications@yourbusiness.com instead of notify@centrx.co, and add a domain for each location if you like. Business and Enterprise connect with one click (coming soon), and other plans follow guided copy-and-paste steps. Without it, email goes out from notify@centrx.co with your business name as the sender. See how to connect your domain.
Is annual billing available?
Yes. Pay yearly and get two months free: a year.
Do I get my own number for texting?
Yes. US carriers require every business to text from its own registered number, so every SMS pack includes a dedicated toll-free number, and we handle the carrier registration for you. You can upgrade to a local number in your area code, or text from the business number your customers already know. See the options under Email and SMS add-ons, or read how to set up your texting number.
Can customers call my business through Centrx?
Yes, with a business phone line: a number customers can call and text, answered right in Centrx. It's $25/month on any paid plan and includes 500 minutes of calls a month, then 4¢ a minute. Enterprise includes the line with 2,000 minutes. You can get a new local or toll-free number, or bring the one you already have.
Can I add more locations?
On Business and Enterprise, yes: each location beyond your plan's included count is $20/month. On Hobby and Pro, moving up a plan is usually the better deal, since each step includes more locations and more of everything else.
Does Centrx include an AI assistant?
Yes. Cenbot, our AI assistant, is built into the panel on every paid plan, with a monthly allowance of replies that grows with your plan: 500 on Hobby, 2,500 on Pro, 10,000 on Business and 50,000 on Enterprise. A reply is each answer or message Cenbot writes. If you use them all, Cenbot pauses until your next month starts, and you can always answer yourself or move up a plan. Ask it how to do something, get answers from your own sales and customer data, or let it reply to customer texts about hours, orders and pickups. It works within each employee's permissions, and texts it sends count toward your SMS pack.
How is my data kept safe?
Every plan has two-factor sign-in, session control, role-based permissions and an audit log. Stripe, PayPal and Square handle card payments, so card numbers never touch Centrx. Read the privacy policy for the details.
How does the Custom plan work?
Apply from Settings → Plan & billing in your Centrx account. Tell us what you need more of and confirm your business details, such as your EIN and state filing number. Our team verifies the business, usually within two business days, and then sets up limits and pricing that fit. Read the full guide.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans have no contract. Cancel from Settings → Plan & billing whenever you like.
