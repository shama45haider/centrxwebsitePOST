Your free trial

Every plan starts with 30 days free, with no card required. Add a payment method in Settings → Plan & billing before the trial ends to keep going. You can switch plans during the trial as often as you like.

Upgrade or downgrade

  1. Go to Settings → Plan & billing.
  2. Choose the plan you want.
  3. Confirm. The change applies right away.

Before downgrading, check you're within the smaller plan's limits, like employees and locations. Confirm what the panel does when over a lower plan's limits

Switch to annual billing

Pay yearly and get two months free: Hobby $490, Pro $990, Business $1,990 or Enterprise $3,299.90 a year. Switch in Settings → Plan & billing. Annual billing also waives setup fees on local and existing texting numbers.

If you reach a plan limit

Checkout is never blocked, so customers can always pay you. You'll get a heads-up at 80% of any limit, so you can upgrade before it matters.

Cancel

Monthly plans have no contract. Cancel any time from Settings → Plan & billing. Confirm what happens to data and access after cancelling
