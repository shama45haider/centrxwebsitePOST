Is Custom right for you?

Custom includes everything in Business, plus:

  • Higher limits on locations, customers, orders and payment links.
  • Volume pricing on email and SMS.
  • A dedicated onboarding and support contact.
  • Monthly or annual billing, priced to fit.

If you're close to a Business limit but not past it, Business is usually the better deal. Talk to sales if you're not sure.

What to have ready

  • Roughly how much you need: number of locations, orders a month and messages a month.
  • Your business type, like LLC or corporation.
  • Your EIN.
  • Your state filing number from your business registration.

Apply

  1. Go to Settings → Plan & billing and choose Apply for Custom. Confirm button name in panel
  2. Tell us what you need more of.
  3. Confirm your business details.
  4. Submit. Your application shows as Under review.

What happens next

Our team checks your business, usually within two business days. If anything doesn't match or we need more detail, we'll contact you.

Once you're approved, your new limits and price go live on your account, sized to the volume you told us.
